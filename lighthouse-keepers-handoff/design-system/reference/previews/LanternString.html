Lantern String

One lantern for every night you finish together. Lanterns never go out: skipping a day, or a week, loses nothing. Every 7th lantern is a big festival lantern that throws a harbour festival at the next Dawn (fireworks-free: bunting, boats with lights, music).

Why not a streak? Streaks punish missed days, which turns practice back into pressure — the exact risk the design doc warns about.
